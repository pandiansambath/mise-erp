## Why August was 4.5× July

Two reasons, and neither is a runaway.

**1. July was not a full month.** The migration to this account happened on
23 July and the EC2 box launched on 29 July, so July only carried about eight
days of infrastructure. August was the first complete month. That alone
accounts for roughly $25 of the increase.

**2. Bedrock: $8.42 in August, $0.48 in September.** That was the AI build and
testing period — the audit, the table assistant, the operator copilot. Broken
down by token type:

| | August | Tokens |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cache-write input | $3.04 | 0.74 M |
| Input | $2.54 | 0.77 M |
| Output | $1.90 | 0.12 M |
| Cache-read input | $0.94 | 2.84 M |

Cache reads are the cheap ones and there were 2.84 M of them, so prompt caching
is working. This was development traffic, not diner traffic, and it has already
fallen back to near zero.

## The one real waste: ECR has 896 old images

This is the finding that matters, and it is entirely self-inflicted.

| Repository | Images | Size | Oldest |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `mise-frontend` | 447 | 52.3 GB | 23 Jul 2026 |
| `mise-backend` | 449 | 51.8 GB | 23 Jul 2026 |
| **Total** | **896** | **104.1 GB** | |

**Neither repository has a lifecycle policy.** Every deploy pushes two images
of roughly 115 MB each and nothing is ever deleted. In seven weeks that has
accumulated 104 GB at $0.10/GB-month.

- Cost today: **$10.17/month**
- Growth: roughly **+$2.50/month**, forever
- It was $2.53 in August and $1.40 in nine days of September — that is the only
  line on the whole bill that is climbing on its own.

Keeping the last 10 images per repository leaves 1.2 GB each and frees
**101.7 GB → saves ~$10.17/month**, which is 28% of the entire run rate. Ten is
already generous; a rollback more than ten deploys old is a rebuild, not a
rollback.

---

## What I recommend, in order

**1. Add an ECR lifecycle policy (saves ~$10/month, ~28%).**
Highest value, zero risk, five minutes. Keep the last 10 images per repo and
expire the rest. Say the word and I will apply it and verify the reclaim.

**2. Get the credit balance and expiry from the console.**
Billing → Credits. This is the only unknown left and it decides everything
about timing. Until credits run out you are paying nothing; the day after, you
are paying ~$26/month with the ECR fix in place.

**3. Raise the budget from $40 to $60, or switch it to actual-spend.**
The `mise-monthly-all` budget at $40 will keep emailing on forecast alone
while the forecaster stays jumpy. Either raise the limit or change the alert to
fire on *actual* spend, which is the number you care about. I would keep a
forecast alert too, just at a level that means something.

**4. Leave RDS and EC2 alone.**
$22 of the $40 baseline is the database and the box, and both are already the
smallest sensible instances. There is nothing to cut there without hurting the
product.

**5. Keep watching Bedrock, but do not act yet.**
August's $8.42 was development. If real diner traffic ever pushes it back to
that level it is worth revisiting the model tiering plan, but at $0.48/month
there is nothing to fix today. The separate `mise-bedrock-ai` budget at $10/month
is the right guardrail and it is nowhere near.
